## Project Summary

Net Analytics is an NBA basketball analytics dashboard that allows users to discover, compare, and analyze team and player performance across seasons. The problem we solve is the lack of a user-friendly platform that brings together NBA data all in one place. Net Analytics will helps users explore, compare, and visualize NBA performance data.
